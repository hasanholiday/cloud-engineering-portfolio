# Travel policy (Northwind Labs, sample)
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Flights over 6 hours may use premium economy with manager approval. Hotels are capped at 200 dollars per night. The daily meal allowance is 60 dollars. Submit receipts within 14 days of returning, or the expense may be declined. Rental cars need approval, and personal car mileage is paid at the company rate.
